    A game inspired by trauma care at Chris Hani Baragwanath Hospital. Simplified rules: this is not medical training.

    How to triage

    Every patient card shows four signs. Each sign has three steps: fine, worrying or critical. The worst sign decides the room. Several worrying signs still don't make a critical one.

    RESUS / ICU: treat now

    • Any critical sign
    • A torso wound (chest or belly) with a fast pulse. It means hidden bleeding inside.

    WARD: treat when there's time

    • Any worrying sign
    • A torso wound or major injury, even if all signs are fine

    WAITING: no doctor needed

    • All signs fine and a minor injury

    Getting worse

    • Untreated patients slide into shock: their signs get worse step by step. Someone who needed the Ward can end up needing Resus.
    • The ❤ heart on each card beats faster as they weaken. A flashing red monitor alarm means they're about to crash.
    • ⏱ shows how long they've been waiting.
    • A ? sign hasn't been measured yet. Wait a moment, or buy better analysis.

    Rooms and doctors

    • Patients in a room appear as round tokens. The ring fills as treatment progresses. Drag a token (or tap it, then tap a room) to move the patient. Moving restarts their treatment.
    • Each room has one lead doctor. Doctors are rated 1–3 in Wounds, Head, Bones and Burns. A skilled doctor treats faster and keeps the patient more stable.
    • A patient in a room below what they need gets no treatment and keeps getting worse. A patient in a room above what they need survives, but blocks a scarce bed.
    • At 08:00 no more patients arrive, but you must still place everyone who came in. Then hand over to the day team.
    • Save 80% to unlock the next night and earn funds for upgrades.
