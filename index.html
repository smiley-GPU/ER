    A game inspired by trauma care at Chris Hani Baragwanath Hospital. Simplified rules: this is not medical training.

    How to triage

    Every patient card shows four signs. Each sign has three steps: fine, worrying or critical. The worst sign decides the room. Several worrying signs still don't make a critical one.

    FineWorryingCritical
    ❤️ PulseNormalFastRacing
    🩸 BPNormalLowVery low
    🧠 AwakeAlertDrowsyUnconscious
    💨 BreathingEasyHardFailing

    RESUS / ICU: treat now

    • Any critical sign
    • A torso wound (chest or belly) with a fast pulse. It means hidden bleeding inside.

    WARD: treat when there's time

    • Any worrying sign
    • A torso wound or major injury, even if all signs are fine

    WAITING: no doctor needed

    • All signs fine and a minor injury

    Watch out

    • The bar under each card is stability. Critical patients crash fastest.
    • Resus has 3 beds and Ward has 4. A minor patient in a Resus bed can block the next gunshot victim.
    • Sending a patient to a room below what they need usually kills them. Sending them higher wastes a bed, but they survive.
    • Survive 80% of a night to unlock the next one. Later nights bring more patients, mass casualties and patients who get worse. Colour hints go away. Injuries can mislead: a "bullet graze" may be harmless, while someone who says "I feel fine" may be bleeding inside. Trust the signs, not the story.
